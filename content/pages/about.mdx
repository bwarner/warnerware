# About Me

I'm Byron, a software engineer and entrepreneur based in San Francisco. With over 20 years of experience in the tech industry, I've had the opportunity to work across a diverse range of companies—from scrappy startups to established enterprises.

## My Journey

My career has taken me through some exciting places:

- **Oncue** - Building the future of home services
- **Healthline** - Making health information accessible
- **OpenTable** - Transforming how people discover and book restaurants
- **Constant Contact** - Helping small businesses with email marketing
- **Gap Inc.** - Retail technology at scale
- **Walmart** - Enterprise e-commerce

Each experience has shaped how I think about building software that truly serves users.

## What I Do

I specialize in **full-stack development**, with deep experience in:

- Frontend development with React, Next.js, and TypeScript
- Backend services and API design
- iOS development with Swift
- Database design and optimization
- Leading engineering teams

I'm passionate about creating elegant solutions to complex problems and building tools that make developers' lives easier.
